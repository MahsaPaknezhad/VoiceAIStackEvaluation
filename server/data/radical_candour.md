# Communicating with Radical Candour

## Overview
Communicating with radical candour matters because it helps foster open communication and trust, leading to psychological safety, and stronger, high-performing teams focused on continuous improvement.

## Definition
**Radical Candour** is:
- Showing you care, acting with empathy and speaking honestly and directly
- How all colleagues should show up in team conversations (e.g., huddles) to help identify root causes and share ideas to improve customer experience

*Source: Scott, K. (2017). Radical Candor, St Martin's Press*

## Communication Styles Framework

### Two-Dimensional Model
- **X-Axis**: Speaking honestly and directly (Low to High)
- **Y-Axis**: Showing care and acting with empathy (Low to High)

### Four Communication Styles

#### 1. Radical Candour (High Care + High Directness)
- **Think**: "I respect the effort behind this idea, and I want to help us get to the best outcome."
- **Say**: "Thanks for sharing that. I have a different take – can I share it so we can explore all angles together?"
- **Result**: Builds trust, encourages conversation, and leads to better outcomes

#### 2. High Care + Low Directness
- **Think**: "I think my idea might not be that important, so I'll just keep it to myself."
- **Say**: "Great ideas everybody!"
- **Result**: Can lead to missed contribution and lower performance

#### 3. Low Care + Low Directness
- **Think**: "I don't really care, and I'd rather not get involved."
- **Say**: "Yeah, whatever works for everyone."
- **Result**: Can lead to missed contribution and disengagement

#### 4. Low Care + High Directness
- **Think**: "This idea doesn't make sense. My ideas are better."
- **Say**: "That idea won't work. My idea is the best and we should just follow it."
- **Result**: Can shut down collaboration and discourage others from sharing

## Practice Framework

### 1. Before
**Prepare**: 
- Think about your thoughts and ideas - what you want to say
- Goal: contribute meaningfully for better outcomes
- Write them down in a sentence or two to help you be clear and focused

### 2. During

#### Show you Care and Act with Empathy:
- Pay attention – this conversation is your current priority
- Seek to understand contributions and perspectives of others, including those different to yours
- Thank, acknowledge, and build/challenge using "yes, and.../ I have a different perspective" as appropriate

#### Speak Honestly and Directly:
- Share your thoughts and ideas with clear, simple language, without attacking others'
- Focus on being curious and achieving shared goals
- Model the behaviour you want to see in others

### 3. After
**Reflect**: Take a few moments to reflect on the conversation
- How did communicating with radical candour impact the conversation and outcomes?
- What challenges did I encounter, and how did I address them?
- What's the most powerful action I can take to improve this skill?

## Reflection Questions

### For Colleagues:
- Think about how you and your colleagues are showing up in team conversations

### For Facilitators/Leaders:
- How is the team showing up? What are you observing?

### For All:
- How might you move yourself and others towards radical candour?
