# Customer Voices Call-back Rubric

| Priority | Criteria | Great looks like | Good looks like | Room to improve looks like |
|----------|----------|------------------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| 4 | Kept customers experience and expectations at centre of the conversation | Conversation anchored back to the original experience and expectations from the customers. | Conversation anchored back to the experience. Expectations were not uncovered. | Conversation did not uncover the experience and did not uncover expectations |
| 1 | Sought to understand and learn more at a deeper level the experience and impact(s), expectations and suggestions. Listened without limiting answers encouraged customer to reflect on experience and respond candidly | Conversation uncovered root causes from the customer on what they experienced, their expectations and their suggestions on how to improve the experience. | Conversation uncovered root causes from the customer on what they experienced, their expectations and their suggestions on how to improve the experience. | Conversation did not uncover more from the customer on what they experienced |
| 7 | Respected customer time by not over-explaining/over-asking | Did not repeat questions that were answered. Kept explanations concise and acknowledged the customers time where appropriate | Did not repeat questions or ask for repeated information. Kept explanations concise | Asked for repeated information or repeat question |
| 3 | Explained purpose of call as learning from their feedback, *without* referring to a "scores", "NPS" or "groups" | Purpose of the call was outlined at the beginning of the call. Customers feedback was mentioned without bringing up specific scores or groups, or the term NPS | Purpose of the call was outlined at the beginning of the call. No acknowledgement of customers feedback. No mention of NPS, scores or groups | NPS, scores or groups were mentioned |
| 2 | Showed interest in responses | Actively listened to the customer. Clarifying or asking for the customer to elaborate on expectations or suggestions on how to improve | Actively listened to the customer | Showed signs of being distracted. Not actively listening |
| 5 | Demonstrated appreciation of customer perspective and commitment to improve their experience | Expressed appreciation of customer perspective. Reinforced the want to improve the customer experience | Expressed appreciation of customer perspective | Did not express appreciation of customer perspective |
| 6 | Ended call with a summary of main points to confirm understanding | Summary of the additional feedback provided. Clarified this has been understood correctly | Summary of the additional feedback provided | No summary |
